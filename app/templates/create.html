Follow one secret from your hands to theirs

Four steps. Each shows what that step can see, and what it can't.

  1. 1 · Your browser

    Locked before it leaves

    you type
    API_KEY=sk_9f2a7c
    uploaded
    7Hq2Vd9xKp0LzRw4mN8bTc2YqF5sJ1eWgA6uZ

    Your browser encrypts it with AES-256-GCM under a fresh random key. Only the encrypted version is sent.

  2. 2 · The link

    The key rides after the #

    /secret/7f3a9c…#Xk9pQ2mNvR8tL4wE7yB1cA5dH

    never sent to the server

    Browsers never send the part after # to a server, so the key travels with the link, not through this site.

  3. 3 · Their sign-in

    You choose who can open it

    signed in
    jane@company.com
    allowed
    jane@company.commatch

    If you listed emails, the recipient signs in and only a verified address on that list can open it. Leave it blank and the link alone opens it.

  4. 4 · Gone

    Opened once, then deleted

    opened
    API_KEY=sk_9f2a7c
    next visit
    nothing here

    Revealing it deletes it in the same step. A second visit gets nothing, and unopened links expire on their own.
